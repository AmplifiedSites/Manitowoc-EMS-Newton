WHO WE ARE

Neighbors helping neighbors.

Newton First Responders have been providing this community with pre-hospital care for 28 years. We strive to provide the best pre-hospital care to those people we help.

Anyone who needs pre-hospital care, emergency or non-emergency in nature, has access to our services 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, at no cost to the patient.

Behind our promise of quality care is our commitment to maintaining the high standards we have set for our organization. Our First Responders are certified by the State of Wisconsin in providing First Responder care and advanced skills.
